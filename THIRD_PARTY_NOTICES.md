## Services and map data

The app calls these services from the browser. Each has its own terms and usage policy, which
apply to anyone running or hosting Trip Recap:

- Map data © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), available
  under the Open Database License (ODbL).
- Map tiles from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/). Satellite imagery and fallback tiles
  from Esri and EOX, under their own terms.
- Road routing from the [OSRM](https://project-osrm.org/) public demo server, which is meant for
  light, non-commercial use.
- Place names and search from [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org/), under the
  [Nominatim usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/), and
  [Photon](https://photon.komoot.io/) as a fallback.

The legacy `backend/` folder depends on Python packages (FastAPI, Pydantic, HTTPX, Shapely,
Playwright, Pillow, pillow-heif and others) that are installed separately and keep their own
licenses.
